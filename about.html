02 A note from Dana

Why I opened Bēsia.

I wanted one place in Accra where extensions were installed the way I was trained to install them: planned around what the hair can carry, and taken out without taking your own hair with them.

Then came the texture system, the first vegan, formaldehyde-free one in Ghana, because I was not willing to offer straightening that cost people their hair. Around both sits the Hair Club, the treatment side of the studio.

DanaFounder, fusion extension specialist

05 The standard

Four things you can hold us to.

01Hair health firstWe look at your scalp and your bonds before we talk about length or colour. If a service would damage your hair, we say so and offer the alternative.
02Your time respectedEvery service carries a published duration and we book to it, so you are seen when you are booked.
03Honest pricingAll 83 prices are published. Where length or density changes the figure, we confirm it before we start, never at the counter afterwards.
04Certified in fusionUK-certified in fusion extensions and trained on every system we run. Your result should come from method, not luck.
