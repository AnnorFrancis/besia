Our Story

A studio built aroundHair Health

Bēsia is Ghana's home of fusion extensions, specialising in seamless KTips and microlinks for results that read as your own hair. Our specialists are UK-certified, and every install is planned around what your hair can carry — not what will sell.

We are also the first studio in Ghana to offer a safe, vegan, formaldehyde-free straightening and texturising system, blending natural ingredients with current technology for healthier hair. As a beauty collaborative, we curate the professionals who work here — so the standard holds whichever chair you sit in.

What We Stand For

Our promise,In Four Commitments

Hair Health First

We assess your scalp and your bonds before we talk about length or colour. If a service would damage your hair, we will tell you and offer the alternative.

Your Time Respected

Every service on our menu carries a published duration, and we book to it — so you are seen when you are booked and you can plan the rest of your day.

Honest Pricing

All forty-seven prices are published. Where length or density changes the figure, we confirm it with you before we start — never at the counter afterwards.

Certified, Not Guessing

UK-certified in fusion extensions and trained on every system we run. Your result should come from method, not luck.
