02 A note from Dana

Hair is cared for before it is styled.

Every appointment at Bēsia starts the same way: we look at the hair you have, and we listen. Density, condition, what you want it to do for the next few months. Only then do we talk about what to install, release, colour or cut.

Our fusion specialists are UK-certified, our texture system is vegan and formaldehyde free, and our scalp work is treatment first. The first consultation is free, and it is the most useful thirty minutes you will spend with us.

DanaFounder, Bēsia Beauty Studio
