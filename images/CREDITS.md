### Note on sourcing
Pinterest was deliberately **not** used. Images there are usually owned by
third parties and re-pinned without a commercial licence, which is a real risk
on a sample sent to businesses. Unsplash's free licence permits commercial use.
When each salon supplies its own photography, replace these files and keep the
same filenames — no code changes needed.
